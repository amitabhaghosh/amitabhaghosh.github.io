I graduated from St. Xavier's College with Honours in English Literature, and completed my Masters in Journalism and Mass Communication from the University of Calcutta. I have been writing in the print media since 2002, and also held a faculty position in Loreto College, Kolkata. The dual experience gave me a platform to come across a diverse range of people and places, interact with young minds, and move in sync with the changing times. My continental shift across the oceans is an evolutionary process, aiding to walk the road hitherto unexplored.

"We are travelers on a cosmic journey, stardust, swirling and dancing in the eddies and whirlpools of infinity. Life is eternal. We have stopped for a moment to encounter each other, to meet, to love, to share. This is a precious moment. It is a little parenthesis in eternity." Paulo Coelho-The Alchemist.

And on this cosmic journey, explorers we are, together. Meet my husband, the adventurous scientist with an artistic twist!
