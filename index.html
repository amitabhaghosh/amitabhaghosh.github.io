My work lies at the intersection of AI, computer networking, distributed systems, and sensor networks — focusing on design and analysis of algorithms, network protocols, modeling, optimization, reinforcement learning, and foundational ML technologies.

At Apple, I help design, architect, and develop generative and agentic systems and networking technologies that power Apple Intelligence across all operating system platforms. I'm privileged to build products that make a meaningful impact on people's lives, helping them realize their dreams. I thrive partnering with cross-functional engineering teams to forge new collaborations, delivering elegant, scalable, and highly performant software and systems in a dynamic, time-sensitive environment.

Previously, I ideated and led various SBIR/STTR projects with Department of Defense (DoD) agencies, winning grants, collaborating with business partners, and helping innovate, build prototypes, and deploy advanced technologies. I was also an adjunct faculty at USC, and served as a TPC member for many IEEE/ACM journals and conferences, and as a panelist at the National Science Foundation (NSF) and the US Army Research Office (ARO).

I got my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the USC Viterbi School of Engineering in 2010, and was a postdoc at Princeton University from 2010 to 2012. At USC, I was part of the Autonomous Networks Research Group, and was advised by Prof. Bhaskar Krishnamachari. At Princeton, I worked with Prof. Mung Chiang and Prof. Jennifer Rexford. In my dissertation, I designed approximation algorithms for multichannel scheduling, power control, and routing topologies to analyze throughput-delay tradeoffs for fast data collection in sensor networks (Thesis).

Prior to getting my Ph.D., I spent four years working on GSM/GPRS technologies at Lucent - Bell Labs, and on ad hoc networks at Honeywell Technologies. I got an M.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Physics from Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur.
