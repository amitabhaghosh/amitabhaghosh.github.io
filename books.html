I have always wanted to maintain a list of books that I read in the recent past or have been reading at present. Here is a first cut of what I remember. My general preference these days is to read science and non-fiction, biographies, politics, technology, and whatever interests me at different points in time. (I am not going to list any of the fictions here, we all read a lot of those).
